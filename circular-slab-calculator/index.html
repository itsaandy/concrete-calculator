How the Circular Slab Estimate Works

The calculator halves the diameter to find the radius, then uses π × radius² × thickness. A 2m diameter slab entered at 100mm thick has a geometric volume of π × 1² × 0.1 = approximately 0.314m³. With 10% selected, the planning quantity is approximately 0.346m³, or 38 bags under the stated yield assumption.

Measure diameter and depth carefully

Measure across the widest internal part of the circular formwork. Use the actual designed thickness, converted to metres by the calculator. If the shape is an annulus or contains large voids, this solid-circle calculator will overestimate unless those volumes are calculated and subtracted separately.

Use a designed thickness

The calculator does not choose thickness, reinforcement, concrete strength or ground preparation for fire pits, water tanks or other loads. Use project documentation or advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

Why add wastage?

Wastage accounts for concrete lost during your project:

  • Uneven ground requiring more concrete in low spots
  • Spillage during pouring and spreading
  • Concrete sticking to mixing equipment
  • Minor measurement variations
10% is standard for most DIY projects.
Use 15% for uneven sites or complex shapes.
