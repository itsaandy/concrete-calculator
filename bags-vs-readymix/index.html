This tool does not guess local ready-mix prices or declare one option the winner. Enter the actual bag shelf price and a written ready-mix quote so the two material totals use the same volume.

Bag calculation

The bag count is volume × 108, rounded up to a whole 20kg bag. The total dry bag weight is the rounded bag count × 20kg. Bag cost is calculated only when you enter a shelf price.

Ready-mix calculation

The ready-mix total is volume × your quoted price per cubic metre, plus the delivery and other quoted fees you enter. Suppliers may apply minimum loads, waiting time, distance, strength-grade, small-load, after-hours or pump charges. Enter the supplier's complete quote rather than relying on a national average.

Cost is only one part of the decision

Access, placement time, available equipment, product specification and the ability to complete a continuous pour can matter as much as the material total. This calculator does not assess those project requirements.
