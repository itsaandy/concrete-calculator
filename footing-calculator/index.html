How the Footing Estimate Works

For rectangular footings, the calculator multiplies length × width × depth × number of footings. It then applies the allowance you select. A 5m × 300mm × 400mm footing entered twice has a base volume of 1.20m³; with 10% selected, the planning quantity is 1.32m³.

Separate unequal footings

The count field assumes every footing has the same dimensions. If your plan contains several footing sizes, calculate each group separately and add the final cubic-metre quantities. Measure trenches after excavation where practical because their real dimensions can differ from nominal dimensions.

Use dimensions from a design

This calculator only estimates material quantity. It does not determine footing width, depth, reinforcement, founding material, concrete strength or whether engineering and approval are required. Enter the dimensions specified in the project documentation.

Why add wastage?

Wastage accounts for concrete lost during your project:

  • Uneven ground requiring more concrete in low spots
  • Spillage during pouring and spreading
  • Concrete sticking to mixing equipment
  • Minor measurement variations
10% is the calculator's default, not a construction rule.
