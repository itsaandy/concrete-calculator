Check the working before you order

Two complete calculation walkthroughs, with diagrams and examples you can change in the tools.

These guides answer measurement and quantity questions. Their diagrams and worked calculations are original, hypothetical examples—not construction plans, completed-project reports or recommended dimensions.

Split an L-shaped outline into separate rectangles, check it by subtraction, apply one allowance and load the result into the project planner.

Turn a volume into bags and a comparable quote

Separate dry bag weight from mixed yield. Check whole-bag rounding and compare a ready-mix quote with clearly labelled hypothetical prices.

How these guides are checked

Andy P, ConcreteCalc's creator and maintainer, is responsible for these guides. Each example exposes its inputs, formula, assumptions and a link that loads the same calculation into a tool. Manufacturer references and review dates are included where a product yield is used.

For formulas and limitations, read the methodology and sources. If you find a mismatch, send the guide URL, inputs and expected result through Contact.
